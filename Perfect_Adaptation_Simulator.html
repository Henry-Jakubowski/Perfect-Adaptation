Perfect Adaptation: A Model with Integral Feedback

Signal S drives synthesis of both a response element R and a second species X. X in turn accelerates degradation of R. Because the steady state of R depends only on the rate constants — not on S — the system's steady-state response is independent of signal strength, even though R responds transiently to every change in S. Notice that R returns to the same steady-state value after each step increase in S — R's transient peak height grows with each step, but its resting value does not. This is the defining signature of perfect adaptation.

About this model BioModels BIOMD0000000312

This ModelBrick describes a simple linear response element supplemented with a second signaling pathway (through species X), creating a response mechanism that exhibits perfect adaptation to the signal. Although the pathway shows a transient response to changes in signal strength, its steady-state response is independent of the signal strength (S). Originally built and simulated in VCell and exported as SBML.
